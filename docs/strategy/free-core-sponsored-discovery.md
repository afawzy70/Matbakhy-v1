# Matbakhy free-core / optional sponsored discovery — exploration
Date: 2026-10-13
Status: OWNER-PROPOSED DIRECTION, not final approved fee schedule, ad implementation, or MVP scope change.
Issue: #5

## Thesis
Make the core marketplace truly easy and free to join for customers and home kitchens. Monetize only opt-in visibility boosts, after there is enough trusted buyer demand that sponsorship can provide measurable incremental value. Do not advertise 'free' if an undisclosed mandatory commission or fee is charged.

## Free baseline (candidate)
Customer: browse, discover, order, track, support without Matbakhy membership fees.
Cook: onboarding/review, public kitchen page, standard listings, availability, order handling and standard organic discovery free. Third-party costs (delivery/payment/optional extras) displayed transparently.

## Optional paid visibility (later)
- Clearly tagged 'Sponsored / إعلان ممول' tiles in home, search or categories.
- Only approved, active, serviceable kitchens with in-stock and currently orderable meals may advertise.
- Initially simple prepaid limited-duration package or capped budget, NOT an opaque auction or complex real-time bidding.
- Sponsored placements never overwrite organic ranking. Keep normal results available, avoid misleading endorsements, and bound ad load.
- Show understandable campaign reporting: display count, click count, recorded attributed orders with reasonable methodology; no guaranteed sales.
- Separate AI assistant organic recommendation quality from ad placement. Any ad in conversational answer must be disclosed as sponsored and genuinely relevant.
- Do not sell identifiable customer info or expose private order details to advertiser kitchens.
- Refund/service and advertiser contracts, invoicing/tax, approval/rejection and abuse review are required before live billing.

## Market entry guardrails
At launch prioritize number of operating kitchens, customer activation, completed orders, repeat rate, no-show exposure, complaints, service quality and contribution margin. Paid advertising cannot create demand by itself. Pilot only when traffic volume offers credible discovery potential.

## Core economic risk
Ads-only may not cover support, trust/safety, OTP, cloud, payment, operations and acquisition costs when marketplace traffic is small. Model unit economics before promising a permanent zero-commission policy. Keep decision on ad-only vs blended commission model OPEN and explicitly owner-controlled.

## Owner decisions later
- Is 0% order commission a firm launch promise or a time-limited promotion?
- Can kitchens buy simple daily/weekly boosts or fixed sponsored slots? What is the value / pricing?
- Which ad surfaces, capped ad frequency and fairness guardrails?
- At what minimum audience / traffic does paid promotion launch?
- Legal and tax counsel review (Egypt) and advertiser terms.
- Reporting attribution and advertiser appeal/credit policies.

## Execution rule
Record concept, do not interrupt the existing MVP plan. No billing, ad feature build, or current default commission removal is authorized by this exploratory proposal alone.
