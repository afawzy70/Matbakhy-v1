# Pilot operating model and readiness

These are operational requirements, not legal advice or a claim of compliance. Owner obtains current Egypt-specific review for food-business licensing/verification, consumer terms, privacy/retention, tax/invoicing and financial arrangements before launch. No rate, legal entity or delivery agreement is assumed.

## Before onboarding real cooks

Define service zones, fulfillment hours, cooking capacity expectations, verification checklist and reviewer, accepted documents/retention, publication policy, ingredient/allergen wording, quality complaint/suspension process, customer support contact and settlement terms. Name responsible operator and backup. Explain what the platform verifies and what it does not guarantee.

Cook onboarding rehearsal: draft → submit → changes requested → resubmit → approval → publish meal → open slot → receive/accept → prepare → delivery or pickup → cash report → statement. Demonstrate rejected/suspended cook cannot sell and private documents remain private.

## Daily operating checklist

1. Review pending cook applications and supply availability for active zones.
2. Monitor overdue acceptance, preparation/fulfillment exceptions and outbox/provider failures.
3. Work support queue by severity; safety matters use immediate escalation.
4. Reconcile previous day's completed/failed orders, cash reports, commission obligations, refunds and remittances.
5. Review unexplained variance, duplicate attempts, suspended supply and alert/backup health.
6. Record shift handover and unresolved cases with owner/next action.

Do not represent planned staffing as actual 24/7 support. Show real hours and expected response times from approved configuration.

## Financial policy to resolve before pilot

Who is merchant/collector, commission basis/rate and rounding, tax treatment, invoice issuer/format, delivery charge recipient, acceptance/cancellation/no-show/failed-delivery rules, full refund eligibility, commission reversal, settlement interval, minimum remittance, evidence requirements and dispute authority. Use sample rates only in synthetic environments. All live changes are versioned, effective-dated and audited.

## Rehearsal pack

Run at least: successful delivery, successful pickup, cook rejection, deadline expiry, customer pre-accept cancel, post-accept cancellation request, last-portion conflict, price change, app timeout/retry, failed delivery, pickup no-show, wrong/missing meal complaint, allergen complaint escalation, disputed cash, full refund, commission reversal and remittance reconciliation. Use customer web and both native apps with cook/ops web. Save exact results and operator feedback.

## Launch go/no-go record

Record approved zones/cooks/limits, operator contacts, policies/versions, provider/environment readiness, exact release artifacts, full gate evidence and owner authorization. Start with bounded order/cook/zone limits; monitor orders and financial variance closely. Disable affected ordering quickly if integrity is in doubt. Expand only with measured capacity and operational support.

## User/operator documentation to deliver with P10

Customer quick start in Arabic/English: account, location, meal/allergens, checkout/cash, status/handover, cancellation/support, privacy/deletion. Cook guide: verification, meals/media, slots/capacity, acceptance deadlines, fulfillment/cash, statements and complaints. Staff guide: permissions, queues, privacy, overrides, reconciliation, configuration and incidents. Developer guide: clean setup/build/test/migration/deploy, provider config, evidence and resume. Validate instructions with a person following them on a clean setup.
